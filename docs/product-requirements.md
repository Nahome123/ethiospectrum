# Product requirements

The launch product is defined by the **Ethiospectrum Engineering-Ready PRD v1.0 (September 29, 2026)** in [prd-v1.md](prd-v1.md). It is the single implementation source of truth. The demo application was reconciled against it feature by feature in [demo-reconciliation.md](demo-reconciliation.md).

Ethiospectrum is a multilingual household service platform for parents and families of children with special needs. It offers three services:

- **RBT Boot Camp:** a self-paced video library with a monthly subscription.
- **Consultation:** $9.99, one time, 60 minutes, one follow-up included.
- **IEP Language Assistance:** $19.99, one time, 60 minutes, English ↔ Amharic and English ↔ Spanish, remote or in person, with written translation and one follow-up included.

Consultation and IEP Language Assistance never require the subscription.

The product must never represent course completion as an RBT certification. It must never add an automatic travel fee or charge an undisclosed fee. It must never treat a service as paid before the payment provider confirms payment. It must never promise benefits, eligibility, representation, or legal, medical, or immigration outcomes.
