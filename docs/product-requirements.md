# Product requirements

Ethiospectrum is a multilingual family-support platform for organizing information, understanding documents, tracking next steps, and finding educational resources in English, Amharic, and Spanish. Implemented product slices include authentication, private household documents, source-grounded document guidance, roadmaps, in-app reminders, support-request and appointment workflows, and household-level Stripe subscription infrastructure. No existing critical feature is paywalled by ETH-028, and billing applies only to a household platform plan—not to support, specialists, appointments, documents, roadmap items, or reminders. Transactional email remains a separate future issue.

The product must distinguish verified document facts from general information, suggested next steps, professional questions, and unknowns. It must never promise benefits, services, eligibility, representation, legal advice, medical advice, immigration advice, or outcomes.
